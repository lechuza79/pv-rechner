# Municipal funding source review — 2026-09-16

## Scope and evidence

The outreach-informed discovery run added 81 source records from 74 municipalities. This review follows those specific sources; it is not a completeness claim about all German municipalities. Climate concepts were classified by document type and relevant funding passages, not audited as legal documents line by line.

The catalogue gains 16 active municipal entries and 8 closed entries. Eight existing entries are corrected, including a missing storage scheme in Recklinghausen. Closed entries remain visible as historical information. A source record, a municipality and a funding programme are different counting units.

Current official pages and linked original guidelines were read on 2026-09-16. Independent reviews are retained with the local research evidence (review-a/b/c/root, council-a/b/c and council-c-final). New geographic keys were checked against the municipal register; new city yields were read from the existing PVGIS service, with no fallback estimates.

## Calculation boundaries

Only supported rules have calculation fields. Building eligibility, special storage chemistry, roof orientation, conditional federal approvals and inseparable cost bases remain explicit information without an automatic deduction. In particular:

- Lüdinghausen: 50% including eligible balcony storage, capped at EUR 500.
- Minden, Kirchlengern and Eppelheim: flat balcony grants of EUR 75, 250 and 75.
- Idstein: roof PV and stationary storage each EUR 100 per unit, each capped at EUR 1,000. The balcony device percentage does not apply to an unseparated battery price.
- Flörsheim: roof PV from 2 kWp, EUR 100/kWp capped at EUR 500.
- Sandhausen: replace the erroneous roof-PV 50% statement with EUR 100/kWp capped at EUR 1,000; storage EUR 100/kWh capped at EUR 600 and limited to 1 kWh/kWp.
- Delmenhorst: remove watt-based tiers from a purchase-price-based historical subsidy.
- Radolfzell: optional advance reservation; the current guideline and programme page say three months for evidence, whereas a press item says two. The guideline is used.

## Combination encoding

Confirmed combinations retain their programme references. An empty list is an explicit prohibition; null records an unresolved or technology-dependent combination. Both prohibited and unresolved combinations remain outside the automatic federal-plus-municipal stack. In particular, Minden requires federal approval for its heating measure, whereas Wendlingen has different rules for balcony equipment and its renovation bonus.

- All new areas checked against the municipal register.
- Real browser checks: balcony overview plus Lüdinghausen, Idstein, Sandhausen, Ottobrunn, Recklinghausen and Flörsheim; visible conditions, amounts and official links inspected.
- Regression cases cover amount caps, geographic boundaries, unresolved eligible cost bases, missing verification, closed rounds and city-page ambiguity.
