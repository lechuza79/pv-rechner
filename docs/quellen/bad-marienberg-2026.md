## Encoding decision

Guideline effective 1 January 2026, not a programme originally created on the January signing date. No end date is invented from the budget-notice publication date.

Annex 3 applies to private current/future owners of self-used one/two-family houses with their main residence in the whole Verbandsgemeinde. Conditions belonging to Annex 1 (old-town location, older buildings) do not automatically apply to Annex 3.

Page 20: heating heat pump 1,000 EUR, hot-water-only heat pump 500 EUR. Both share the table conditions: existing heating at least two years old, professional installer, hydraulic balancing, high-efficiency circulation pump and GEG requirements. These heating conditions do not cover storage.

Storage position 3.8 states exactly “100 EUR / Kilowatt pauschal, maximal 600 EUR”. Do not convert this to EUR/kWh or an unconditional 600 EUR grant. The combined technology label does not establish an independent balcony-module subsidy. Retain textual historical information only, no structured calculation fields, even on accidental reactivation.

Footnote 3 prohibits combination with EU/federal/state/other municipal funding for position 3.8 only. Section 9 otherwise permits combination subject to the other funding body's conditions. A blanket federal-combination flag for the mixed programme would be unsupported, hence `combinableWith: null`.

Application before binding contract; start normally after approval, with expressly permitted written early-start authorisation (7.3). One year for implementation, three months for proof after completion, five-year binding period, no entitlement and limited budgets. The card links the official complete conditions.
