# Funding and municipality solar discovery — first linkage

Read-only snapshot, 10 September 2026. No publishing or production writes.

## Coverage
168 programme records: 153 municipal, 8 district, 5 state, 2 federal. 48 have a catalogue start date; 37 of those match exactly one municipality name. In total 137 programme regions have a unique exact municipality-name match. This is a provisional linkage, not validated geographic coverage: district programmes, municipal associations, aliases and homonymous municipalities require explicit scope mapping. A programme can subsidize technologies other than balcony solar. The machine-readable companion retains all records for auditing, not all as eligible comparison cases.

149 history records cover observations from 18 August to 9 September 2026. Observation date is not programme start or effective change date. Thus this history cannot retrospectively establish 2025 policy changes. Dates stored in programme fields require source verification and distinction between first launch, renewal and application window.

## Two contrasting candidates
Aulendorf catalogue start: 1 July 2025. Balcony unit counts: April 10, May 10, June 12, July 23, August 18, September 9, October 6, November 9, December 7. Candidate: temporary increase coinciding with the recorded start. July is the highest recorded month in the available series. No July 2024 comparator is present. August–December 2024 totals 38, same months 2025 totals 49; this descriptive increase does not isolate funding from other changes. Start date could not be independently confirmed in the accessible page text during this pass. Source to verify: https://www.aulendorf.de/wirtschaft-energie/foerdermassnahmen . Do not publish a causal claim or treat the stored date as verified.

Königswinter catalogue start: 1 May 2025. January–April counts 10,28,39,43; May–August 26,24,43,58. There is no immediate positive step in May. This is a counterexample to automatically turning every programme start into a positive-growth story, NOT evidence that the programme failed. Source date still to verify: https://www.koenigswinter.de/de/foerderprogramm-klimaschutz/foerderung-steckersolar.html .

Hamburg catalogue start: 1 October 2025, targeted at low-income households. Whole-city counts cannot measure take-up in that eligible group. Counts October–December 2024: 242,137,108; 2025:175,176,149. Aggregate autumn total changes 487 to 500, hiding different monthly directions. Do not infer programme effectiveness from that total.

## Consequences for discovery
1. Keep known funding as an enrichment, not a candidate exclusion. The existing finder excludes locations with known funding to avoid asking a question with a supposed known answer; that incorrectly treats programme presence as an explanation.
2. Add an event marker only for verified effective dates, with programme eligibility and technology scope. Show the same monthly axis for data and funding context.
3. Compare periods and reference municipalities only after source vintages and geographic coverage are reconciled. Missing catalogue entry means unknown funding exposure, not no funding.
4. Distinguish automatic descriptive copy (counts, dates, changes) from editorially checked contextual copy (funding launch/change). Causal wording requires evidence beyond these aggregates.
5. Keep the large-project story family separate and valid: a single large commissioning event is interesting independently of broad adoption or subsidies.

## State
The linkage run is complete; it produces candidates and explicit gaps, not a validated treatment-effect estimate. Shared monthly/annual export provenance remains unresolved. No programme data were edited, no legacy detector changed. The next empirical steps are effective-date verification for the short list and same-export monthly regeneration; unverified control groups must not be invented.

## Source verification completed later on 10 September
A targeted domain search retrieved the relevant municipal text that the earlier page extraction missed. Aulendorf explicitly states a new incentive programme effective 1 July 2025 and EUR 150 per household for plug-in solar: https://www.aulendorf.de/wirtschaft-energie/foerdermassnahmen . Its separate announcement distinguishes the 2026 continuation from the 2025 launch: https://www.aulendorf.de/stadt-aulendorf/aktuelles/aus-aulendorf/869/kommunales-foerderprogramm . The earlier missing source confirmation is now resolved for date and amount.

Aulendorf 2025 balcony units sum to 125 / 157.23 kWp monthly and 125 / 157.22 kWp annually, within rounding. July's 23 is the highest of the twelve months. This supports a descriptive coincidence story, not a treatment-effect claim. A twelve-month visual with programme-start marker, source links, copy, teaser and social mockup is now in the concept lab. Same-export provenance remains a publication task.
